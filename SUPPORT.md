# Helenite support

Helenite is developed by **Egress**. Community review and release acceptance are still pending. A review build, issue form or signed installer does not mean that public subscriptions or the community listing are available.

## Start here

- [English installation, connection and recovery guide](docs/USER-GUIDE.md)
- [한국어 설치·연결·복구 안내](docs/USER-GUIDE.ko.md)
- [Website guide](https://helenite.net/guide)

Keep your existing account, paired devices and vault settings when troubleshooting. Do not delete plugin settings or repeatedly register a new PC. Keep a separate backup of your vault before recovery actions. Downloaded notes remain local files; reinstalling or signing in cannot recover a lost encryption key.

## Report an installation or sync problem

Use this repository's **Bug report** form for a problem that can be described publicly with disposable example notes. English or Korean is welcome. During testing, you can also contact your test coordinator.

Include:

- Desktop app version, OS version, and the Obsidian and Helenite plugin versions **on each affected device**. The desktop diagnostics report does not include the running plugin version.
- What you did, what you expected, what happened, and the approximate time and time zone. Use dummy names and text in reproduction steps.
- Whether the host PC was awake and online, Helenite and Obsidian were running there, and Obsidian was visible on the receiving device. State Wi-Fi or cellular without network names or addresses.
- The error message with personal details removed. Do not paste a raw log or attach a vault.

Never post connection QRs, invitation links or codes, login codes, passwords, keys, tokens, plugin `data.json`, account or billing identifiers, private note contents or device/network addresses. Check all text before submitting; do not attach screenshots containing these details.

Desktop **Connection help → View diagnostics → Copy diagnostics** is optional. Review its preview, then send it privately only when requested. It is not automatically uploaded and does not prove a phone received a note. Do not substitute raw plugin files or logs for that report.

## Private contact

For privacy, account, billing or sensitive support requests, email **admin@egress.llc**. Start with a short description; do not send credentials, a vault, identity documents or payment card details. If identity verification or more information is needed, we will arrange the appropriate next step. This document does not promise a response time.

Security concerns belong in the [private security reporting process](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
